How to use it on GitHub

3 simple steps and your site goes live on your own subdomain.

1

Open Pages Settings

Go to your GitHub repository → Settings → scroll down to Pages.

2

Add Custom Domain

Paste your generated subdomain into the Custom domain field and press Save.

3

Enable HTTPS

Wait a few minutes, then tick Enforce HTTPS. Your free subdomain is now live 🎉
